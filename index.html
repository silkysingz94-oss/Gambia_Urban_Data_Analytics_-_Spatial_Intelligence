Methodology and limitations

Spatial statistics depend on source data, geographic identifiers, measurement units and reference year. Area-level statistics should not be interpreted as individual-level characteristics.
Consider the modifiable areal unit problem (MAUP), ecological fallacy, boundary edge effects, missing observations and self-reporting bias. Use consistent geographic boundaries when comparing census years.
